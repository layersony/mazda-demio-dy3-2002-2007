C-PILLAR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091700459100

1. Remove the tire house trim. (See TIRE HOUSE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Partially peel back the seaming welt.

3. Remove the upper anchor of the rear side seat belt.

4. Remove the cover, then remove the bolt.

5. Pull the C-pillar trim outward, then disengage clips A and pins B.

6. Remove the C-pillar trim.

7. Install in the reverse order of removal.
