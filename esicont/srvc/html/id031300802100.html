DRIVE SHAFT PRE-INSPECTION

id031300802100

1. Inspect the connections for any looseness.

• If there is any malfunction, tighten or replace the part.

2. Inspect the boot for damage and cracks.

• If there is any malfunction, replace the part.

3. Inspect the drive shaft for bends, cracks, and wear in the joint or splines.

• If there is any malfunction, replace the part.
