POWER BRAKE UNIT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id041100801800

Caution
•  Once the brake switch clearance has automatically been adjusted, it cannot be adjusted again. Therefore, replace the switch with a new one when replacing the power brake unit or performing any procedure that changes the pedal stroke.

1.  Remove the front wiper and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2.  Remove the cowl panel. (See COWL PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3.  Remove the front suspension tower bar. (See FRONT SUSPENSION TOWER BAR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4.  Remove the windshield wiper motor. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER MOTOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5.  Remove the master cylinder. (See MASTER CYLINDER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6.  Remove in the order indicated in the table.

am2zzw00006715

1
Vacuum hose
2
Joint pin
(See BRAKE PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
3
Nut
4
Power brake unit
5
Gasket

7.  Remove the brake switch. (See BRAKE PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

8.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

9.  After installation,inspect the shift-lock and key interlock. (ATX)
