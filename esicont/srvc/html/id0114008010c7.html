FUEL PUMP UNIT DISASSEMBLY/ASSEMBLY [ZY]

id0114008010c7

Warning
• Fuel line spills and leakage are dangerous. Fuel can ignite and cause serious injuries or death and damage. Fuel can also irritate skin and eyes. To prevent this, do not damage the sealing surface of the fuel pump unit when removing or installing.

1. Follow "BEFORE SERVICE PRECAUTION" before performing any work operations to prevent fuel from spilling from the fuel system. (See BEFORE SERVICE PRECAUTION [ZY].)

2. Disassembly in the order indicated in the table.

1
Fuel pump body
2
Pressure regulator
3
Fuel pump
4
Fuel filter (low-pressure)

3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
