DTC P2504

Charging system voltage high

DETECTION CONDITION

• The PCM determines that the generator output voltage is 18.5 V or more or battery voltage is 16.0 V or more while the engine running.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Short to power supply in wiring harness between generator connector terminal D and PCM connector terminal 2AF
• Battery malfunction
• PCM malfunction
