RESTRAINTS FEATURES

id080000100200

Improved safety
• Driver-side air bag module adopted
• Passenger-side air bag module adopted
• Curtain air bag module adopted
• Side air bag module adopted
• Three-point seat belt with the following functions for front seat passengers adopted
- ELR (Emergency Locking Retractor: emergency locking mechanism)
- Pre-tensioner seat belt (See PRE-TENSIONER SEAT BELT CONSTRUCTION/OPERATION.)
- Load limiter, which adjusts restraint force of the seat belt to reduce the possibility of injury to passengers caused by excess seat belt pressure after pre-tensioner operation
• Three-point seat belt with the following functions for rear seat passengers adopted
- ELR
- ALR (Automatic Locking Retractor: child-restraint seat locking mechanism)
