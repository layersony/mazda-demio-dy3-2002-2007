TRANSAXLE OIL INSPECTION [F35M-R]

id0515008000a1

1. Park the vehicle on level ground.

2. Stop the engine and allow approx. 3 min before continuing.

3. Remove the vehicle speedometer sensor (without ABS)/hole cover (with ABS). (See VEHICLE SPEEDOMETER SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION (WITHOUT ABS) [F35M-R].)

4. Verify that the oil level is as shown in the figure.

• If the oil level is lower than the low level, add the specified amount and type of oil through the vehicle speedometer sensor installation hole.
Manual transaxle oil
Grade: API Service GL-4 or GL-5
Viscosity (all-season): SAE 75W-90
Viscosity (above 10 °C {50 °F}): SAE 80W-90

5. Install the vehicle speedometer sensor (without ABS)/hole cover (with ABS). (See VEHICLE SPEEDOMETER SENSOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION (WITHOUT ABS) [F35M-R].)
