ENGINE OIL REPLACEMENT [ZY]

id0111008004c0

Warning
• Hot engines and engine oil can cause severe burns. Turn off the engine and wait until it and the engine oil have cooled.
• Continuous exposure with USED engine oil has caused skin cancer in laboratory mice. Protect your skin by washing with soap and water immediately after this work.

1. Position the vehicle on level ground.

2. Remove the oil filler cap.

3. Remove the oil pan drain plug, and drain the engine oil.

4. Install the oil pan drain plug with a new washer.

Oil pan drain plug tightening torque
30-41 N·m {3.0-4.2 kgf·m, 22-30 ft·lbf}
Note
• The amount of residual oil in the engine can vary according to the replacement method, oil temperature, etc. Verify the oil level after engine oil replacement.

5. Fill with the following type and amount of engine oil.

Recommended engine oil

Grade (API)
SG, SH, SJ, SL
ILSAC
GF-2, GF-3
Viscosity (SAE)
40, 30, 20W-20, 20, 10W-30, 10W-40, 10W-50,
20W-40, 15W-40, 20W-50, 15W-50, 5W-30, 5W-20

Engine oil capacity (approx. quantity)
Oil replacement: 3.7 L {3.9 US qt, 3.3 Imp qt}
Oil and oil filter replacement: 3.9 L {4.1 US qt, 3.4 Imp qt}
Total (dry engine): 4.2 L {4.4 US qt, 3.7 Imp qt}

6. Install the oil filler cap.

7. Start the engine and inspect for oil leakage.

• If the oil leaks, specify the faulty part and repair or replace it.

8. Inspect the oil level.
