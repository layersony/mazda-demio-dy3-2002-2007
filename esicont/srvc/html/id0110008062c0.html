IDLE SPEED ADJUSTMENT [ZY]

id0110008062c0

Caution
• Changing the TAS while attempting to adjust the idle speed could cause an engine malfunction. Do not change the TAS adjustment position.

1. Complete the engine tune-up preparation.

2. Verify that the ignition timing is within the specification. (See IGNITION TIMING INSPECTION [ZY].)

3. Verify that the idle speed is within the specification using the RPM DATA MONITOR function.

Idle speed
600-700 rpm
