DTC P0480

Cooling fan relay No.1 control circuit malfunction

DETECTION CONDITION

• The PCM monitors the cooling fan relay No.1 control signal. If the voltage remain low or high, the PCM determines that the cooling fan relay No.1 control has a malfunction.
Diagnostic support note
• This is continuous monitor (other)
• The MIL does not illuminate.
• The PENDING CODE is available if the PCM detects above malfunction condition during the first drive cycle.
• The FREEZE FRAME DATA is available.
• The DTC is stored in the PCM memory.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Cooling fan relay No.1 malfunction
• Connector or terminal malfunction
• Open circuit wiring between the ignition switch and cooling fan relay No.1 terminal A
• Open circuit in wiring between cooling fan relay No.1 terminal E and PCM terminal 1AV
• Short to ground in wiring between cooling fan relay No.1 terminal E and PCM terminal 1AV
• Short to power circuit between cooling fan relay No.1 terminal E and PCM terminal 1AV
• PCM malfunction
