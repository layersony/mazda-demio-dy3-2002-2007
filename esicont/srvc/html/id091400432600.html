REAR OUTER HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091400432600

1. Remove the rear door latch and lock actuator.

2. Remove the bolt.

3. With the rear side of the rear outer handle fixed and the outer handle lever pulled out (1), remove the rear side of the rear outer handle from the rear door.

4. Pull out the front side of the rear outer handle from the rear door.

5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
