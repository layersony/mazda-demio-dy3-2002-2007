NO.12 MD PLAYER DOES NOT EJECT THE MD

id0903e1824300

12

MD player does not eject the MD

Possible DTC:07:Er01

Troubleshooting hints
• MD malfunction
• MD panel malfunction
• MD panel improperly installation
• Poor connection of MD player connector or terminal (e.g., damaged, bent, pulled-out pin, corrosion)
• MD player malfunction

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1
• Is there MD IN on the display?
Yes
Go to Step 3.
No
Go to the next step.
2
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Inspect the connection of the MD player connector.
• Inspect both MD player connector and wiring harness-side connector for poor connection (such as damaged/bent/pulled-out pins, corrosion)
• All the pins and connector normal?
Yes
Replace the MD player.
No
If the MD player connector/pin is malfunctioning:
• Replace the MD player.
If the wiring harness-side connector/pin is malfunctioning:
• Repair or replace the pins and/or the connector.
3
• Depress eject button on the MD panel.
• Is the eject button normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Replace the MD panel.
4
• Visually inspect the MD panel.
• Is the MD panel normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Replace the MD panel.
5
• Is the MD panel installed properly?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Install the MD panel properly.
6
• Visually inspect the MD.
• Is the MD normal?
Yes
Replace MD player.
No
Explain to the customer that the defect MD cannot be used.
