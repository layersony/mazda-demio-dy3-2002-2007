CENTRIFUGAL BALANCE CLUTCH OUTLINE [FN4A-EL]

id0517a1001300

• A centrifugal balance clutch mechanism, which cancels the centrifugal oil pressure, has been adopted to improve clutch control.
• A bonded seal piston (press-worked component of a piston and a seal) has been adopted for each clutch and brake to reduce the piston size and weight.
