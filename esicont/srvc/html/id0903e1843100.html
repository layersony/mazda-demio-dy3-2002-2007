id0903e1843100

7

LCD does not display at all/Possible DTC: 09:Er20, 21:Er19

Troubleshooting hints
• Audio unit malfunction
• Center panel module malfunction.

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1
• Turn the audio unit to ON.
• Press both POWER ON/OFF and SEEK UP buttons for 1.0 s or more at the same time.
• Does the LCD display properly?
Yes
Replace the audio unit.
No
Replace the center panel module.
