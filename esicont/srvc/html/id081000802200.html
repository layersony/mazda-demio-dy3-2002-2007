CLOCK SPRING INSPECTION

id081000802200

1. Remove the clock spring. (See CLOCK SPRING REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Inspect for continuity between the clock spring terminals.

• If there is no continuity, replace the clock spring.

Note
• When the vehicle-side connector of the clock spring is disconnected, terminals 3J and 3L are shorted to prevent unexpected deployment of the air bag module. When it is connected, the terminals are open.
