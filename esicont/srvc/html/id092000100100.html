ENTERTAINMENT OUTLINE

id092000100100

• A center panel module composed of the installed audio unit with the audio switch, A/C switch, hazard warning switch and rear window defroster switch built into the center panel has been adopted.

• The audio unit consists of the following parts:

- Base unit (AM/FM tuner and CD player/changer)
- Lower module (cassette deck or MD player)
- Cover

• A base unit (CD player or CD changer) and lower module (cassette deck or MD player) are options.

• Module availability depends on vehicle grade.

• The audio control switch is equipped on the steering wheel for audio operation.

• A center roof antenna has been adopted.

• Four speakers installed as standard, one each to the front and rear doors, have been adopted.

• All information related to the audio system appears on the center panel LCD.
