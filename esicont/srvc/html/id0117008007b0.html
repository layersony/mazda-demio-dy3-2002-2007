BATTERY RECHARGING [ZY]

id0117008007b0

Warning
• Hydrogen gas is produced during normal battery operation. A battery-related explosion can cause serious injury. Keep all flames (including cigarettes), heat, and sparks away from the battery, otherwise evaporated gas from the battery fluid may catch fire, and cause serious injury or death.
Caution
• When disconnecting the battery, remove the negative battery cable first and install it last to prevent damage to electrical components or the battery.
• To prevent damage to electrical components or the battery, turn all the accessories off and stop the engine before performing maintenance or recharging the battery.
• Do not quick charge for over 30 min. It will damage the battery.
• To avoid deformation or damage to the battery, remove the battery plugs while charging the battery.

1. Remove the battery and then place it in a pan of water.

2. Remove the battery plugs.

3. Connect a battery charger to the battery and adjust the charging current as follows.

Battery slow charge current
55D23L (48): 4.8-6.0 A
Battery quick charge current [30 min.]
55D23L (48): 30 A

4. Verify that the it is within the specification after completing the battery recharging, and that the voltage remains the same more than 1 h after recharging.

• If it is less than the specification, replace the battery.
Voltage
12.4 V or more
