HEADLINER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

id091700458300

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Partially peel back the seaming welt and weatherstrip.

3. Remove the A-pillar trims. (See A-PILLAR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4. Remove the B-pillar upper trims. (See B-PILLAR UPPER TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5. Remove the C-pillar trims. (See C-PILLAR TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

6. Remove the interior light. (See INTERIOR LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

7. Remove the cargo compartment light. (See CARGO COMPARTMENT LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

8. Remove the sunvisors. (See SUNVISOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

9. Remove the assist handles. (See ASSIST HANDLE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

10. Disconnect the roof harness connector, remove the clip, and remove the connector.

11. Disconnect the rear washer hose.

Caution
• The rear washer hose may contain residual washer fluid. After disconnecting the rear washer hose, drain any remaining washer fluid from the hose.

12. Remove the fasteners.

13. Pull out the rear center seat belt and remove the headliner.

14. Take the headliner off from the liftgate opening.

15. Install in the reverse order of removal.
