DOOR OUTLINE

id091100103500

• The inner construction of the door, including parts such as the power window regulator, inner handle, door speaker, harness, have been integrated into a single door unit.
• Due to the integration of the door unit, and overall door weight reduction has been achieved.
• Waterproofing of the inner door unit parts has been achieved due to sectional design.
• Fiberglass reinforced plastics have been adopted for the door unit to improve the rigidity.
• A ripple-shaped side impact bar has been adopted to improve rigidity in case of a collision.
